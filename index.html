What is this

A SHARED
CANVAS
FOR ALL

Live Canvas is a real-time multiplayer pixel art experience — inspired by Reddit's r/Place. Every registered participant gets access to a shared 200×200 grid. Place one pixel every 30 seconds.

Watch as collective creativity transforms a blank white canvas into something none of you planned — a living record of this moment.
